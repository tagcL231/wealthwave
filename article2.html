7 Dividend Stocks That Pay You Every Month

📅 June 3, 2026⏱ 6 min read✍️ WealthWave Team

What if your money paid you every single month — like a salary — without you doing any extra work? That's exactly what monthly dividend stocks do. Here are 7 of the best ones to consider.

💡 Monthly dividends = 12 paychecks per year from your investments. Reinvest them and watch compound interest explode your wealth.

The 7 Best Monthly Dividend Payers

1. Realty Income (O) — "The Monthly Dividend Company"

Dividend Yield: ~5.1% | Paid dividends every single month for 50+ years. Owns 13,000+ properties worldwide. One of the most reliable dividend stocks ever created.

2. AGNC Investment Corp (AGNC)

Dividend Yield: ~14% | A mortgage REIT that pays extremely high monthly dividends. Higher risk, higher reward. Best for income-focused investors.

3. Main Street Capital (MAIN)

Dividend Yield: ~6.8% | A Business Development Company (BDC) that lends to small businesses. Pays monthly AND occasional special dividends. Excellent track record.

4. EPR Properties (EPR)

Dividend Yield: ~7.5% | Owns entertainment properties like movie theaters, ski resorts, and education facilities. Recovered strongly post-pandemic.

5. Gladstone Investment (GAIN)

Dividend Yield: ~6.2% | Another solid BDC with a long history of monthly payments and occasional bonuses. Great for passive income portfolios.

6. LTC Properties (LTC)

Dividend Yield: ~6.0% | Invests in senior housing and healthcare facilities. Strong demographic tailwind as population ages. Very stable income.

7. Stag Industrial (STAG)

Dividend Yield: ~4.1% | Industrial REIT owning warehouses and distribution centers. Benefits from the eCommerce boom. Lower yield but much safer.

How Much Can You Earn?

If you invest $10,000 across these 7 stocks evenly at an average yield of 7%, you'd earn approximately $700/year or $58/month in passive income — while still owning the stocks.

Scale to $100,000 and that becomes $583/month in completely passive income. That's a car payment, utility bills, or groceries — paid by your investments.

Important: Always do your own research before investing. Dividend yields can change and past performance doesn't guarantee future results.
